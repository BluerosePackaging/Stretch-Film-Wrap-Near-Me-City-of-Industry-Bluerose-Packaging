## Start With the Load You Are Wrapping

There is no single stretch film that works equally well for every pallet. The products being wrapped and the way the film will be applied both matter.

Before choosing a film, consider:

* Total pallet weight
* Product shape and size
* Sharp corners or edges
* Whether the load is uniform or irregular
* Hand or machine application
* Expected transportation conditions
* Indoor or outdoor storage
* Required level of load containment
* Frequency of wrapping

## Choosing Between Hand and Machine Wrap

One of the first decisions is how the film will be applied.

**Hand stretch film** can be practical for lower-volume operations, individual pallets, irregular loads, and situations where workers need greater control over the wrapping process.

**Machine stretch film** is better suited to operations where pallets are wrapped frequently and automated equipment is already part of the packaging line.

**Pre-stretched film** can provide another option for manual wrapping operations where ease of application and controlled film usage are important considerations.

Yes. Hand film is designed for manual application, while machine film is intended for use with pallet-wrapping equipment.

### How do I know which stretch film is right for my pallet?

Consider the load's weight, shape, dimensions, wrapping method, storage conditions, and transportation requirements. These factors can help determine the appropriate film type and specifications.

### Can stretch film be used on irregular pallets?

Yes. Certain stretch film types can be suitable for irregular loads, although the film should be selected according to the load characteristics and wrapping process.

### Is colored stretch film available?

Yes. Colored stretch film can be used for visual identification, organization, privacy, or product differentiation.
